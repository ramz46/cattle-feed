Precision Ration Formulation & Analysis

Balancing fermentable carbohydrates, bypass amino acids, and physically effective fiber to maximize income over feed cost (IOFC).

Nutritional Engineering

Eliminate Nutrient Waste. Maximize Bulk Tank Yields.

Feeding dairy cows based on generic crude protein numbers wastes thousands of dollars in excess nitrogen excretion and strains liver metabolism. Our formulation service evaluates feeds down to individual amino acid pools and hourly rumen degradation rates.

Using the Cornell Net Carbohydrate and Protein System (CNCPS v6.55), our nutritionists model your forage inventory against herd production metrics, ambient temperature, days in milk (DIM), and body condition scores (BCS).

Metabolizable Methionine & Lysine Targeting: Unlocking milk protein synthesis without overfeeding expensive soybean meal.
Subacute Acidosis Prevention: Strict calculation of peNDF (physically effective NDF) to maintain 550+ daily cud-chewing minutes.
Fresh-Cow DCAD Optimization: Anionic salt balancing to ensure blood calcium remains steady through freshening.
Step-by-Step Delivery

Our Ration Formulation Process

A structured, data-driven methodology that ensures consistency from forage bunker to feeding alley.

01. Sampling

Silage & Forage Core Sampling

Representative multi-point core samples gathered from silage bunks, wrapped round bales, and commodity sheds.

02. NIR Lab

Wet Chemistry & NIR Testing

Analysis for dry matter, soluble protein, neutral detergent fiber (NDF), 30-hour NDF digestibility, and mycotoxins.

03. Formulation

CNCPS Dynamic Modeling

Computer simulation balancing energy, limiting amino acids, rumen buffers, and trace mineral requirements.

04. Verification

TMR Mixer Shaker Box Audit

Penn State Particle Separator audit at the feed bunk to ensure zero sorting by cows and consistent ration delivery.
