Precision Rumen Microbiology

Engineered to Support High Metabolic Demands

Modern dairy cows face immense physiological stress during peak lactation. Our precision feeds balance rumen degradable and undegradable proteins while shielding rumen pH against acidosis.

Rumen-Bypass Methionine & Lysine

Provides the exact limiting amino acids directly to the small intestine for optimal milk protein synthesis.

Live Microencapsulated Yeasts

Scavenges ruminal oxygen to foster cellulolytic bacteria and maintain steady anaerobic fermentation.

Organic Chelated Minerals (Zn, Cu, Mn, Se)

Up to 3x higher bio-absorption to drastically lower somatic cell counts (SCC) and accelerate hoof healing.
