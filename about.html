Our Heritage

Built From the Soil Up by Dairy Farmers & Veterinarians

What began as a localized agricultural feed cooperative in 1994 has expanded into an internationally certified livestock nutrition powerhouse serving over 14,000 dairy farms across the continent.

We recognized early on that commercial dairy genetics were progressing faster than standard feed store rations. High-producing cows producing 30+ liters of milk daily required precision amino acid balancing, protected bypass fats, and micro-minerals that conventional steam pelleting simply degraded.

Today, our automated twin-screw processing mill produces over 45,000 metric tons of specialized cattle rations each month, each batch rigorously tested using real-time Near-Infrared (NIR) spectroscopy to ensure zero mycotoxin contamination and 100% label guarantee.

Milestones

Three Decades of Agricultural Innovation

From our first batch of balanced calf starter to automated bulk pneumatic tanker fleets, discover our journey.

1994

Cooperative Foundation & First Mill

Founded in Des Moines, Iowa by three bovine veterinarians and five local dairy operators frustrated with variable grain quality and recurrent acidosis.

2004

Launch of Chelated Mineral Technology

Introduced the BoviMax line of organic glycinate-bonded trace minerals, drastically improving first-service conception rates across commercial partner herds.

2014

Pneumatic Bulk Silo Delivery Fleet

Expanded logistics to a dedicated fleet of pressurized bulk feed trucks capable of direct-to-silo discharge without grain fracturing or dust loss.

2026

Ultra-Lact Pro NIR Dynamic Formulation

Integrated real-time forage sensor data with smart feed mills to automatically adjust ration energy and protein density according to daily seasonal variations.
